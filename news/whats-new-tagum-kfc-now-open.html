What’s New Tagum: KFC Now Open!

KFC has opened in Tagum City, serving its chicken favorites, meals, burgers, and sides. The location is on the National Highway near BIBU Square, in front of the Water District.

October 19, 2026 at 11:19 AM· Tagum City, Davao del Norte
Nag open na jud ang ginahulat nato na KFC here in the city! Tagumenyos can now enjoy KFC’s crispy fried chicken, rice meals, burgers, and iconic bowls. If you were to ask me what’s my favorite here’s a few I can recommend - Original Recipe Chicken - KFC Famous Bowl - Chicken Crispy Tenders with FLC dip - KFC Chicken Sandwich - Mashed Potatoes, Coleslaw and Gravy They are open from 7am to 11pm and soon will open 24hrs 💻KFC 📍National Highway, Tagum City near BIBU Square and fronting Water District
