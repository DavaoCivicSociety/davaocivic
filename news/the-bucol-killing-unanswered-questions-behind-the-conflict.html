📰 THE BUCOL KILLING — UNANSWERED QUESTIONS BEHIND THE CONFLICT

On the evening of November 25, 2025, Barangay Captain Oscar "Dodong" Bucol of Tres de Mayo, Digos City, was fatally shot while reportedly conducting a Facebook Live broadcast.

October 18, 2026 at 6:15 PM· Digos City, Davao del Sur
Digos City, Davao del Sur — October 10, 2026   INTRODUCTION On the evening of November 25, 2025, Barangay Captain Oscar "Dodong" Bucol of Tres de Mayo, Digos City, was fatally shot while reportedly conducting a Facebook Live broadcast. What initially appeared as a single act of violence has since unfolded into a complex web of long-standing disputes, political realignments, conflicting documents, and missing evidence — leaving the community with more questions than answers.   I. ROOTS: A DISPUTE LONG BEFORE THE POLITICS The conflict did not begin on the night of the killing. Long before political figures became involved, there was already deep-seated tension between the Bucol family and the Cagas family over land matters, road projects, and jurisdiction within Tres de Mayo. Video footage captures a heated exchange between Captain Bucol and Didi Cagas, mother of former Vice Governor Marc Cagas IV. In the exchange, Bucol asserts his authority: "I am the Barangay Captain. This is my jurisdiction." The dispute involved questions over contracts and negotiations within the barangay. During these exchanges, Captain Bucol also made serious public allegations against Marc Cagas — claims regarding his alleged involvement in illegal drugs and associations with questionable individuals. These allegations have never been officially investigated or substantiated by authorities.   II. DISQUALIFICATION AND THE FINAL PEACE VISIT Tension escalated following a COMELEC decision disqualifying Marc Cagas from his position. Amid this charged atmosphere came what would be the final recorded interaction. According to accounts, on the day before the killing, Marc Cagas brought Captain Bucol to visit Didi Cagas — who was already bedridden. The meeting was presented as a peace gesture, an attempt to settle their differences. Captain Bucol reportedly believed reconciliation had been reached. The following evening — November 25, 2025 — he was dead.   III. THE INCIDENT AND THE MISSING WEAPONS Police reports indicate that Marc Cagas was present at the scene shortly after the shooting. Authorities filed three complaints against him on November 28: Direct Assault, Grave Threat, and Alarm and Scandal — relating to his alleged conduct toward responding officers. But it is what happened that same day that has raised the most eyebrows: On November 28, 2025 — the very day charges were filed — Marc Cagas submitted an Affidavit of Loss reporting two high-powered sniping rifles missing from his possession. ⚠️ INCONSISTENCIES IN THE RECORDS Document Reported Date & Time Affidavit of Loss November 28, 2025 — 12:00 midnight Police Blotter Entry November 27, 2025 — 4:35 PM Details such as serial numbers, ammunition specifications, and license expiration dates also differ between documents. Authorities have described this as a "clerical error." THE BALLISTIC QUESTION The weapons described as "missing" are not ordinary firearms. Sniping rifles are designed for long-range, high-precision shooting — the same characteristics consistent with how Captain Bucol was killed. Yet: - ✅ The weapons are no longer available for forensic examination - ✅ No official PNP Crime Laboratory ballistic report has been released to the public - ✅ The public statement that there is "no direct evidence linking him" came from the PRO 11 Regional Director — not from independent ballistic testing or the crime lab itself   IV. WHAT THE PUBLIC IS ASKING These are not conspiracy claims — these are legitimate questions that demand clear answers: 1. If the weapons were never used in the crime — why were they not voluntarily turned over for testing? Declaring them "lost" removes the very evidence that could prove innocence. 2. Why the conflicting dates? If this was truly a simple clerical error — where is the original, unaltered record? 3. What was the true purpose of the peace visit the day before the killing? If genuine reconciliation was underway — why was Captain Bucol killed less than 24 hours later? 4. Were the drug allegations ever investigated? Captain Bucol made serious public claims — if false, why were they not publicly disproven? If true — what implications does this have? 5. Who benefits most from Bucol's death? In any investigation, this is the most basic question — and one that remains unanswered.   V. OFFICIAL STATEMENT VS. PUBLIC DEMAND On the record, PNP authorities have stated that the investigation remains active. However, no comprehensive findings have been released to the public. What the community demands is straightforward: - 🔓 Release the complete, unredacted police blotter and incident reports - 🔓 Account for the two missing sniping rifles — serial numbers, last seen locations, circumstances of loss - 🔓 If any ballistic testing was conducted — release the full report from the PNP Crime Laboratory, not a summary statement - 🔓 Continue the investigation independently — free from political influence   VI. CONCLUSION Captain Dodong Bucol was a public servant serving his community. His killing demands more than silence, partial answers, or conveniently missing evidence. Every discrepancy, every delayed report, every unanswered question erodes public trust — not just in this case, but in the promise that justice applies equally to all citizens, regardless of last name or position. "Justice is not served when questions are avoided. It is served when evidence is complete, transparent, and made public. The people of Tres de Mayo, Digos City, and all of Davao del Sur deserve nothing less." — End —   ✅ KEY POINTS FOR PUBLIC SHARING Headline Options: - "Two Rifles Vanished, One Killing Unresolved: The Bucol Case" - "Documents Don't Match, Weapons Gone, Questions Remain" - "Justice For Bucol: What We Know, What We Still Need Answers To" Social Media Hashtags: #JusticeForDodongBucol #WhereAreTheRifles #FullTransparencyNow #DigosCityTruth #BallisticReportNow   ⚠️ IMPORTANT NOTE: This article presents facts gathered from publicly available documents, police reports, and witness accounts.
