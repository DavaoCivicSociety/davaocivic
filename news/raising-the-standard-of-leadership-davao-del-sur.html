Raising the Standard of Leadership

A long-term vision for Davao del Sur focused on inclusive growth, agricultural innovation, responsible investment, stronger public services, and opportunities for the next generation.

October 19, 2026 at 11:14 AM· Davao del Sur
#ELEVATION | Davao del Sur Leadership is not just about what we accomplish today. It is about where we want Davao del Sur to be 5, 10, or 20 years from now. Dili lang “Unsay atong buhaton karon?” kundili “Asa nato dad-on ang atong probinsya?” True progress goes beyond roads, buildings, and infrastructure. It means creating opportunities, strengthening agriculture, attracting responsible investments, improving public services, and building a future where our youth can thrive without leaving home. Digos City can become more than a commercial center. It can be a hub of innovation, agri-processing, and inclusive economic growth—connecting farmers to markets, transforming local produce into globally competitive products, and creating sustainable livelihoods. But development must never stop at the city limits. Ang tinuod nga kalamboan dili lang makita sa kauswagan sa sentro, kondili sa kausaban nga maabot sa matag lungsod, barangay, ug pamilya. Because leadership is not measured by how much we build, but by how many lives we improve and how well we prepare the next generation. Vision. Innovation. Collaboration. Elevation. This is how we raise the standard of leadership. This is how we elevate Davao del Sur. #ELEVATION #RaisingTheStandardOfLeadership #DavaoDelSur
