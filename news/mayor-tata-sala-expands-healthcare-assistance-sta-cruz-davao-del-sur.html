Mayor Tata Sala Expands Healthcare Assistance in Sta. Cruz, Davao del Sur

Sta. Cruz has expanded the Santa Cruz Lingap Program, offering qualified residents assistance with hospitalization, medicines, laboratory procedures, dialysis, and funeral expenses through local healthcare and service-provider partnerships.

October 20, 2026 at 9:45 AM· Sta. Cruz, Davao del Sur
STA. CRUZ, Davao del Sur — The municipal government of Sta. Cruz has expanded its healthcare assistance program through the Santa Cruz Lingap Program, strengthening access to medical services for residents, particularly those facing financial difficulties. Under the leadership of Mayor Jose Nelson “Tata” Sala, the local government has partnered with healthcare institutions and service providers, including Davao Doctors Hospital, Brokenshire Medical Center, Mercury Drug, other pharmacies, and funeral service providers to broaden the assistance available to qualified beneficiaries. Through the program, eligible residents may receive financial assistance for hospitalization, medicines, laboratory procedures, dialysis treatments, and funeral expenses, subject to assessment and program requirements. The municipal government said the initiative forms part of its continuing efforts to strengthen healthcare delivery and ease the financial burden on families dealing with medical emergencies and other health-related expenses. Funded by the municipal government, the Santa Cruz Lingap Program underscores the local administration’s commitment to providing accessible, responsive, and people-centered healthcare services to the community.
