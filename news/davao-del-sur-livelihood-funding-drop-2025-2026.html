₱21.8 MILLION IN 2025, JUST ₱5.2 MILLION IN 2026: WHY DID DAVAO DEL SUR’S LIVELIHOOD BUDGET DECLINE?

DAVAO DEL SUR | October 23, 2026

The reported allocation for livelihood programs in Davao del Sur dropped from ₱21.8 million in 2025 to ₱5.2 million in 2026—a difference of ₱16.6 million, or approximately 76.1%.

The figures raise important questions about the provincial government's spending priorities and its continuing support for families seeking sustainable livelihood opportunities.

What explains the reduction, and how will the province ensure that livelihood programs continue to serve communities that need them?

📉 A significant reduction that deserves explanation

Livelihood assistance can help farmers, small business owners, and other residents through financial support, equipment, skills training, and opportunities to develop sustainable sources of income.

Given the reported decline, the public deserves to understand which programs were affected and whether alternative funding sources are available to maintain these services.

Key questions include how many beneficiaries received assistance in 2025 compared with 2026, which programs experienced budget reductions, and how much funding was actually spent in each year.

🏗️ Infrastructure and livelihood: where is the balance?

The Provincial Government of Davao del Sur also reportedly implemented infrastructure projects worth approximately ₱1.06 billion from January to September 2026.

Roads, bridges, and public facilities are essential to economic development. They can improve transportation, connect communities to markets, and make public services more accessible.

However, development must also consider the economic opportunities available to ordinary families.

Infrastructure can create the conditions for economic growth, but people also need opportunities to earn, establish businesses, and build sustainable livelihoods.

The challenge is to ensure that infrastructure investments and livelihood programs complement each other in advancing the province's development goals.

🔎 Questions the provincial government should address

To clarify the reported reduction, the provincial government should provide answers to the following questions:

Why did the reported livelihood allocation decline from ₱21.8 million in 2025 to ₱5.2 million in 2026?

Which livelihood programs were affected by the budget changes?

How many beneficiaries received assistance in each year?

Were alternative funding sources provided to sustain livelihood programs?

What plans are in place to support families, farmers, and small entrepreneurs who depend on these initiatives?

Answers should be supported by official budget documents, expenditure reports, and program accomplishment records.

It is also important to distinguish between approved budget allocations and actual expenditures. A reduction in the allocation does not automatically mean that actual assistance declined by the same proportion, particularly if other funding sources or program adjustments were involved.

📌 Public spending must deliver public benefits

Residents have a legitimate interest in understanding how public funds are allocated and how government programs affect their lives.

Transparency means making financial information accessible. Accountability means explaining spending decisions and demonstrating results.

Ultimately, the issue is not whether infrastructure development should continue. It is whether the province's overall spending priorities adequately address both physical infrastructure and the economic needs of its people.

Development should not be measured solely by the number of roads built or facilities completed, but also by the opportunities created and the lives improved.

The reported decline in livelihood funding deserves a clear, evidence-based explanation so the public can better understand the province's priorities and the future of its livelihood programs.
