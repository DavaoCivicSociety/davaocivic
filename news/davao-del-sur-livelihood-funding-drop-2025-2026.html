DAVAO DEL SUR: WHY DID LIVELIHOOD FUNDING DROP FROM ₱21.8 MILLION TO ₱5.2 MILLION?

A reported decline in livelihood funding from ₱21.8 million in 2025 to ₱5.2 million in 2026 raises questions about budget priorities, program support, and transparency in Davao del Sur.

October 19, 2026 at 3:25 PM· Davao del Sur
By: Davao del Sur TV | October 19, 2026 A reported 76% reduction in livelihood funding has raised questions about the Provincial Government of Davao del Sur’s priorities for 2026. Based on the budget figures cited, the allocation for livelihood programs declined from ₱21.8 million in 2025 to ₱5.2 million in 2026—a difference of ₱16.6 million. For families who depend on livelihood assistance, skills training, and small-business support, this raises an important question: What happened to the funding, and how will the province sustain its livelihood programs? The province has also reported ₱1.06 billion worth of infrastructure projects implemented from January to September 2026, covering 186 projects, with 66% reportedly completed and turned over. Infrastructure is important. Roads and public facilities can improve connectivity, create economic opportunities, and make essential services more accessible. But development must also consider the people who depend on government programs to improve their lives. WHERE IS THE BALANCE? A reduction in the livelihood allocation does not automatically mean that assistance to beneficiaries has declined by the same amount. Other funding sources or changes in program implementation may explain the difference. Nevertheless, the public deserves a clear explanation. Which livelihood programs experienced budget reductions? How many beneficiaries received assistance in 2025 compared with 2026? Were alternative funding sources provided? What plans are in place to support struggling families, farmers, and small entrepreneurs? These questions deserve answers supported by official budget documents and program accomplishment reports. DEVELOPMENT MUST REACH THE PEOPLE The issue is not whether Davao del Sur should invest in infrastructure. The province needs roads, bridges, and public facilities. The question is whether infrastructure development is accompanied by sufficient investment in livelihood opportunities, healthcare, education, and social protection. A province may build roads to connect communities, but its people also need opportunities to earn, access essential services, and build sustainable livelihoods. Public spending should therefore be assessed not only by the amount allocated or the number of projects completed, but also by the improvements it delivers to ordinary residents. THE QUESTION THAT DESERVES AN ANSWER Why did the reported livelihood allocation fall from ₱21.8 million in 2025 to ₱5.2 million in 2026? The provincial government can help clarify the issue by publishing a detailed comparison of the two years’ budgets, identifying changes in funding sources, explaining program adjustments, and reporting the number of beneficiaries served. Transparency allows the public to understand the government’s decisions. Accountability helps ensure that those decisions respond to the needs of the people. Because progress is not measured by infrastructure alone. It is also measured by whether families have a fair opportunity to improve their lives. DAVAO DEL SUR TV — Local News. Real Stories.
